Real recorded sounds used in the workout app preview
====================================================
All of the following allow use in a personal web app. CC0 / public domain
(no attribution required). Credit is included here anyway.

The app clips (real_bell_*_start/end.mp3, real_clapper.mp3) were trimmed and
loudness-normalized (~-14 LUFS, true peak about -1 dBFS), converted to mono
64 kbps MP3. Sample sequences (real_bell_N_sample.mp3) are start at 0s,
clapper at 1.8s, end at 2.6s.

Additional fight-bell candidates (not wired into the app yet)
=============================================================
Larisa preferred option 3 (Freesound 520998, “Fight bell”): a bright, sharp,
high-pitched metal bell with a fast triple ring. The four clips below are
more real recordings with a similar character, processed the same way
(mono, two-pass loudnorm about −14 LUFS / −1 dBFS, 64 kbps MP3 under 100 KB).
Each sample (fight_N_sample.mp3) uses the same sequence as real_bell_3_sample.mp3:
start ding at 0 s, wooden clapper at 1.8 s, triple at 2.6 s.

Spectral notes versus 520998’s first ding (peak 4521 Hz, centroid ~3738 Hz,
decay to −40 dB about 2.25 s, triple spacing 242 / 234 ms):
